# Suspicious Transaction and Regulatory Reporting Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains what happens when a fraud case is escalated to
Compliance and which regulatory reports FinAI Bank files with FINTRAC.

## 2. Receiving escalated cases

When a fraud officer escalates a case (FRD-003 §6.2), Compliance receives the
case with the AI investigation summary, the officer's notes and the evidence.
A Compliance analyst must begin the review within **1 business day**.

## 3. Reasonable grounds to suspect

A Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) is required when there are **reasonable
grounds to suspect** that a transaction, or attempted transaction, is related
to money laundering or terrorist financing. Examples include:

- Structuring payments to avoid thresholds (FRD-005 §7).
- Money-mule patterns (FRD-005 §6).
- Activity inconsistent with the customer's profile with no credible
  explanation.
- Transfers involving elevated-risk jurisdictions without a clear purpose.

Fraud against the customer (for example a romance scam) may also produce an
STR when the proceeds are being laundered through the receiving accounts.

## 4. Report types and deadlines

| Report | Trigger | Deadline |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) | Reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF; any amount, including attempted transactions | As soon as practicable after the grounds are established |
| Large Cash Transaction Report (LCTR) | Cash of CAD 10,000 or more received in one or more transactions within 24 hours | Within 15 calendar days |
| Electronic Funds Transfer Report (EFTR) | International EFT of CAD 10,000 or more, sent or received, in one or more transactions within 24 hours | Within 5 working days |
| Terrorist Property Report | Property known or believed to be owned or controlled by a listed terrorist group | Immediately |

## 5. Who may file

Only the Chief AML Officer or a delegate in the Compliance team may decide to
file an STR. Fraud officers and AI agents recommend escalation; they do not
file reports.

## 6. Tipping-off prohibition

Employees must **never** tell a customer, or anyone outside the Compliance
process, that a suspicious transaction report has been, or may be, filed.
Customer-facing staff and the Customer Support Agent must use neutral wording
such as *"Your payment is under a routine security review."*

## 7. Record keeping

Copies of every report and the supporting analysis are kept for at least
**5 years** (CMP-004).
