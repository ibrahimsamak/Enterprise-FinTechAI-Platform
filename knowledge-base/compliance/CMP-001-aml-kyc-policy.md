# Anti-Money Laundering and Know Your Customer Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how FinAI Bank prevents its services from being used for
money laundering or terrorist financing. It applies to all customers, products
and channels, and to all employees and automated systems, including AI agents.
FinAI Bank is a reporting entity under Canada's *Proceeds of Crime (Money
Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act* (PCMLTFA) and reports to FINTRAC.

## 2. Customer identification (KYC)

Every customer must be identified before an account is opened, using one of
the accepted methods:

- Government-issued photo identification, verified in person or through the
  bank's digital identity verification.
- The dual-process method (two reliable sources, such as a credit file and a
  utility bill).

For business customers, the bank must also confirm the business's existence
and identify its **beneficial owners** (anyone owning or controlling 25% or
more).

## 3. Customer risk rating

Every customer is assigned a risk rating at onboarding and reviewed on events
(such as a flagged payment) and periodically:

| Rating | Examples | Review frequency |
|--------|----------|------------------|
| Low | Salaried resident, domestic activity only | Every 3 years |
| Medium | Regular international payments, small business | Every 2 years |
| High | PEP, cash-intensive business, links to elevated-risk jurisdictions | Every year |

## 4. Enhanced due diligence (EDD)

EDD is required for high-risk customers and includes: confirming source of
funds and source of wealth, senior-management approval to keep the
relationship, and enhanced ongoing monitoring of transactions.

## 5. Politically exposed persons (PEP)

A PEP is someone who holds or held a prominent public function, or a family
member or close associate of such a person. Foreign PEPs are always high risk.
Domestic PEPs and heads of international organizations are high risk when
other risk factors are present.

## 6. Ongoing monitoring

Transactions are monitored continuously against the customer's expected
activity. Activity inconsistent with the customer profile - such as sudden
high-value international transfers from a domestic-only customer - must be
reviewed, and escalated to Compliance when unexplained.

## 7. AI agents and AML

AI agents may summarize evidence and recommend escalation, but **decisions to
file a report, exit a customer or freeze funds are made only by authorized
Compliance staff**.
