# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FinAI Bank collects, uses, shares and protects your
personal information, in line with Canada's *Personal Information Protection
and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA).

## 1. Information we collect

- **Identity and contact**: name, date of birth, address, phone, email,
  identification documents.
- **Financial**: accounts, balances, transactions, payees, income and credit
  information.
- **Device and usage**: device identifiers, IP address, approximate location
  and app activity, used mainly to protect your account from fraud.
- **Communications**: messages and calls with us, including conversations with
  our AI assistant.

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide and manage your accounts and services.
- To verify your identity and meet our legal obligations, including anti-money
  laundering laws.
- To detect and prevent fraud - for example, by checking whether a payment
  matches your usual activity.
- To improve our services, using aggregated or de-identified data where
  possible.
- To offer you products, only if you have agreed to receive offers.

## 3. Use of artificial intelligence

We use AI tools to help answer your questions and to help our staff review
payments for fraud. These tools only access the information needed for the
task, under the same access rules as our employees. **AI tools do not make
final decisions** to decline a payment or close an account - a person at
FinAI Bank does. You can ask to speak with a person at any time.

## 4. Sharing your information

We share personal information only:

- With service providers that work for us under contracts requiring them to
  protect it.
- With credit bureaus, when you apply for credit and on an ongoing basis for
  credit products.
- With regulators, law enforcement or others when required or permitted by law.
- With your consent.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Access**: you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about
  you. We respond within **30 days**.
- **Correction**: you can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
- **Marketing**: you can withdraw consent to marketing at any time in the app.
- **Withdrawing consent** for other uses may limit the services we can provide;
  some uses are required by law and cannot be refused.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or
as required by law - generally at least **5 years** after an account is
closed, to meet record-keeping obligations.

## 7. Safeguards

We protect information with encryption, access controls, monitoring and staff
training. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will
notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## 8. Contact

Questions or complaints: FinAI Bank Privacy Office, through the app or at a
branch. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office
of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
