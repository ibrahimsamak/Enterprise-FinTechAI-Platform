# Opening, Closing and Securing Your Account

## 1. Who can open an account

You can open a personal account with FinAI Bank if you are at least the age of
majority in your province (18 or 19), or a minor with a parent or guardian as
joint holder, and you live in Canada.

## 2. Identification required

You can open an account online or in a branch. We verify your identity using
either:

- One valid government-issued photo ID (for example a driver's licence or
  passport), verified through our app or in person, **or**
- Two documents from different reliable sources (for example a credit file and
  a utility bill, or a bank statement from another bank).

We may also ask for your occupation and the expected use of the account. This
is required by Canadian law.

## 3. Closing an account

You can close an account at any time through the app, by phone or in a branch.
Before closing:

- Make sure all cheques and pre-authorized payments have cleared or been moved.
- Transfer or withdraw your remaining balance.

Closing an account within 90 days of opening it costs $20 (see FEE-001 §6).
Otherwise closing is free.

## 4. Protecting your online banking

- Use a unique password for FinAI Bank that you do not use anywhere else.
- Turn on two-step verification and app notifications.
- Never share your password, PIN or one-time passcode - **FinAI Bank will never
  ask you for them** by phone, email or text.
- Be careful with links in emails and texts. Log in by typing our address or
  using the app.
- Keep your phone number and email up to date so we can reach you about a
  payment review.

## 5. If you think your account is compromised

Call us right away (24/7) or lock your online banking from the app. We will
secure your account, remove unknown devices, and help you reset your
credentials.

## 6. Online Banking Security Guarantee

FinAI Bank will reimburse you 100% for losses from transactions made through
online or mobile banking that you did not authorize, provided that you:

- Kept your password, PIN and one-time passcodes confidential,
- Did not install software that gave someone remote control of your device at
  their request, and
- Reported the unauthorized activity to us promptly after you noticed it.

The guarantee does not cover payments you made yourself, even if you were
tricked into making them. If you think you were the victim of a scam, contact
us immediately - we will try to recall the funds.
