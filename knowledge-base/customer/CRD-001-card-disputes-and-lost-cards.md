# Card Disputes, Lost and Stolen Cards

## 1. Lost or stolen card

If your FinAI debit card is lost or stolen:

1. **Lock your card** immediately in the app (Cards → Lock card). Locking is
   instant and can be undone if you find the card.
2. If the card is not found, report it as lost or stolen in the app or call
   us 24/7. The card is cancelled and a replacement is mailed within
   **5–7 business days**.
3. Review your recent transactions and report any you do not recognize.

The first replacement card each year is free (see FEE-001 §6).

## 2. Unauthorized transactions

You are not responsible for transactions you did not make or authorize, as long
as you:

- Reported the loss, theft or unauthorized use promptly (within 30 days of the
  statement showing the transaction), and
- Did not share your PIN or voluntarily reveal it, and did not contribute to the
  unauthorized use.

Reimbursement of confirmed unauthorized transactions is normally made within
**10 business days** of your report.

## 3. Disputing a transaction

You can dispute a card transaction when, for example:

- You were charged twice for the same purchase.
- You did not receive goods or services you paid for.
- The amount charged differs from the amount you agreed.
- A cancelled subscription kept charging you.

How to dispute:

1. Try to resolve it with the merchant first.
2. Open a dispute in the app (Transactions → select transaction → Dispute) or
   call us, within **90 days** of the transaction date.
3. Provide any receipts, emails or cancellation confirmations.

Most disputes are resolved within **45 days**. In some cases a temporary credit
is applied while we investigate; it may be reversed if the dispute is not
upheld.

## 4. Card status

You can see whether your card is active, locked, cancelled or on its way in the
app under Cards. Contactless and online purchases can be switched on or off
individually.
