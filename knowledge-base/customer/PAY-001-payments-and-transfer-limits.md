# Payments, Transfers and Limits

This document explains how payments work at FinAI Bank, how long they take, the
limits that apply, and why a payment may be held for a security review.

## 1. Daily limits

Limits reset at midnight Eastern Time and apply per customer.

| Payment type | Default daily limit | Maximum on request |
|--------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Interac e-Transfer | $3,000 | $10,000 |
| Bill payments | $10,000 | $50,000 |
| International wire (online) | $10,000 | $50,000 |
| International wire (branch) | No fixed limit (subject to review) | - |
| Debit card purchases | $3,000 | $5,000 |
| ATM withdrawals | $1,000 | $2,000 |

To raise a limit, contact us through the app or visit a branch. Higher limits
may require identity verification.

## 2. Processing times

| Payment type | Typical arrival |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Transfers between your FinAI accounts | Immediate |
| Interac e-Transfer | Usually within 30 minutes |
| Bill payments | 1–3 business days |
| International wire | 1–5 business days, depending on the destination and intermediary banks |

International wires sent after 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time, or on weekends and
holidays, are processed the next business day.

## 3. Why a payment may be held for a security review

To protect your money, some payments are held for an additional security
review before they are released. This can happen, for example, with large
payments, payments to a new recipient, or payments abroad. A review is routine
and does not mean you did anything wrong.

- Most reviews are completed within a few hours.
- We may call you on the phone number we have on file to confirm the payment.
  We will **never** ask for your password or a one-time passcode.
- If we cannot reach you, the payment stays on hold until we can confirm it.

## 4. Cancelling or recalling a payment

- Interac e-Transfers can be cancelled while they have not been deposited.
- Pending international wires can be cancelled free of charge while they are on
  hold or not yet sent.
- A wire that has already been sent can only be **recalled**, which requires
  the receiving bank's cooperation and is not guaranteed. The recall fee is
  listed in FEE-001 §3.

## 5. Information needed for an international wire

- Recipient's full name and address.
- Recipient's bank name, SWIFT/BIC code and account number or IBAN.
- Purpose of the payment.
- Amount and currency.

Always confirm new or changed bank details directly with the recipient using a
phone number you already know - payment-detail changes sent by email are a
common fraud method.
