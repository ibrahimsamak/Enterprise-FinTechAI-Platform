# Personal Banking Fee Schedule

All fees are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and apply to personal accounts at
FinAI Bank from January 1, 2026. Fees are charged to the account on which the
service is used.

## 1. Chequing account plans

| Plan | Monthly fee | Waived when minimum daily balance is at least | Included transactions |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Essential | $4.00 | $1,500 | 15 per month |
| Everyday | $11.95 | $3,000 | Unlimited |
| Premium | $29.95 | $6,000 | Unlimited, plus 2 free outgoing international wires per month |

On the Essential plan, each transaction above the 15 included costs **$1.25**.
Students (with valid enrolment) and seniors aged 60+ receive a **$4.00
discount** on any plan's monthly fee.

## 2. Savings accounts

FinAI savings accounts have **no monthly fee**. One withdrawal or transfer out
per month is free; each additional one costs **$5.00**. Transfers between your
own FinAI accounts are always free.

## 3. Transfers and payments

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Interac e-Transfer (sent) | Free on Everyday and Premium; counts as a transaction on Essential |
| Interac e-Transfer (received) | Free |
| Bill payment | Free (counts as a transaction on Essential) |
| Outgoing international wire (online) | $35.00 |
| Outgoing international wire (branch) | $50.00 |
| Incoming international wire | $15.00 |
| Outgoing domestic wire | $25.00 |
| Wire recall or amendment | $25.00, plus any fees charged by other banks |

International wires may also incur fees from intermediary and receiving banks,
which are deducted from the amount sent and are outside FinAI Bank's control.

## 4. Foreign exchange

When a payment, card purchase or ATM withdrawal is in a foreign currency, it is
converted at the FinAI Bank posted rate, which includes a **2.5%** conversion
markup. The rate is set at the time the transaction is processed.

## 5. ATM and cash

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| FinAI Bank or partner ATM in Canada | Free |
| Other ATM in Canada | $2.00 (the ATM owner may charge an extra fee) |
| ATM outside Canada | $5.00 plus the 2.5% conversion markup |

## 6. Account services

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Non-sufficient funds (NSF) - item returned | $10.00 |
| Stop payment on a cheque or pre-authorized debit | $12.50 |
| Paper statement (per month) | $2.00 (e-statements are free) |
| Replacement debit card | Free once per year, then $10.00 |
| Bank draft | $9.95 |
| Account closure within 90 days of opening | $20.00 |

## 7. Fee changes

FinAI Bank gives at least **30 days' notice** of any new fee or fee increase by
message in online banking and on our website. You may change plans or close
your account without a closure fee during that notice period.
