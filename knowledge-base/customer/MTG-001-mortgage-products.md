# Residential Mortgage Products and Qualification

## 1. Mortgage types

| Type | How it works | Best for |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Fixed rate | Interest rate and payment stay the same for the whole term. | Predictable budgeting |
| Variable rate | Rate moves with the FinAI prime rate; payment stays the same and the portion going to principal changes. | Borrowers comfortable with rate changes |
| Open | Can be repaid in full at any time with no penalty; higher rate. | Short-term needs, planned sale |
| Closed | Lower rate; prepayment above the allowed amount has a penalty. | Most homeowners |

Terms are available from 1 to 10 years. The usual maximum amortization is
**25 years**, or **30 years** for insured first-time buyers and buyers of newly
built homes, and for uninsured mortgages with at least 20% down.

## 2. Down payment

- With less than **20%** down, mortgage default insurance is required and its
  premium is added to the mortgage.
- The minimum down payment for an insured mortgage is **5%** of the first
  $500,000 of the purchase price and **10%** of the portion above $500,000.
- Insured mortgages are available for purchase prices below **$1.5 million**.
  At or above that price, the minimum down payment is 20%.

## 3. Qualifying (stress test)

To qualify, you must be able to afford payments at the **minimum qualifying
rate**, which is the higher of:

- Your contract rate plus 2 percentage points, or
- 5.25%.

We also look at your debt service ratios: housing costs should generally not
exceed **39%** of gross household income, and total debt payments should not
exceed **44%**.

## 4. Prepayment privileges (closed mortgages)

Each year, without penalty, you can:

- Pay up to **20%** of the original principal as a lump sum, and
- Increase your regular payment by up to **20%**.

Unused privileges do not carry forward to the next year.

## 5. Prepayment penalties

If you pay more than your privileges allow, or break a closed mortgage before
the end of its term:

- **Variable rate**: 3 months' interest.
- **Fixed rate**: the greater of 3 months' interest or the interest rate
  differential (IRD).

Ask us for a penalty estimate before deciding; it depends on your balance,
rate and remaining term.

## 6. Renewal

We send a renewal offer at least **21 days** before your term ends. You can
renew with FinAI Bank, switch to another lender, or pay off the balance at
maturity without penalty.

## 7. Applying

Apply online or with a mortgage specialist. You will need proof of income,
proof of down payment (with 90 days of account history), identification, and
the purchase agreement once you have one. A pre-approval holds a rate for
**120 days**.
